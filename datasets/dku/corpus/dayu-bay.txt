Dayu Bay commercial center

Dayu Bay is a short walk from the DKU campus. The commencement visitor guide gives the walking distance as 500 meters. Visitors may park for free in the Dayu Bay commercial center parking lot.

The guide says an array of restaurants is located there. This local note does not name any restaurant, does not list opening hours, and does not list prices.
