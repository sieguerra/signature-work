Duke University, Durham, North Carolina (not the Kunshan campus)

Duke University's home campus is in Durham, North Carolina, United States, on nearly 9,000 acres. Duke traces its roots to 1838 and officially became Duke University in 1924. Duke enrolls more than 15,000 students in undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs.

Duke University Libraries (including Perkins and Bostock on West Campus) are in Durham. They are not the DKU Library at No. 8 Duke Avenue, Kunshan. This note does not list Perkins closing times, because those hours are not copied here.

DKU is described on Duke's international activity list alongside Duke-NUS in Singapore.
