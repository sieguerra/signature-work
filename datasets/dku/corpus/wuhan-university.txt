Wuhan University (partner institution, not the DKU campus)

Wuhan University is in Hubei province in central China. It is a partner in Duke Kunshan University. It is not in Kunshan and is not the DKU library, dining halls, or registrar.

Wuhan University's history is traced to the Ziqiang Institute founded in 1893 by Zhang Zhidong. In 1928 the institute was renamed Wuhan National University.

This note does not list DKU class dates, DKU library hours, or Wuhan University library hours.
