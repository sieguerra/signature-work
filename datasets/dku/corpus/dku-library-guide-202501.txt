DKU Library Guide (January 2025 brochure)

This document copies service hours and space facts from the January 2025 DKU Library Guide PDF. Where it disagrees with the later Locations & Hours website, treat the website as current and this brochure as dated January 2025.

Library building: Monday-Sunday 08:00-23:00. Side gates use swipe access. The guide lists 23 self and group study rooms and 560 seats.

1st floor information desk in this brochure:
Monday-Thursday: 09:00-21:00
Friday: 09:00-17:30
Saturday: 14:00-18:00
Sunday: 14:00-20:00

The Tea House is on the 3rd floor and connects to a sky garden. The Meditation Room is on the 4th floor (LIB4-16) and looks over the Academic Quad.

Source: https://library.dukekunshan.edu.cn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Duke-Kunshan-University-Library-Guide-202501.pdf (January 2025 guide; retrieved 2026-09-10)
