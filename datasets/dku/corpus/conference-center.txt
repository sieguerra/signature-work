Duke Kunshan University Conference Center

The Conference Center is on the DKU campus in Kunshan. It has over 200 guest rooms, a gym, and on-campus parking. The auditorium holds up to 300 guests. The grand ballroom can be divided into two rooms.

Dining venues named for Conference Center guests include the Conference Center Restaurant, Executive Dining Restaurant, and Water Pavilion. A campus restaurant and a Family Mart in the Community Center are also mentioned.

This note does not list room rates, a weekday shuttle timetable, or Halal Dining meal times (those times are on the commencement dining note).
