Campus dining — Academic Building

Three dining halls on campus serve Chinese, Western, and Halal dishes. This note covers only the two Academic Building venues.

University Dining @ Academic Building:
Breakfast 07:00-09:30
Lunch 11:00-13:30
Dinner 17:00-19:30

Executive Dining @ Academic Building:
Lunch 11:00-13:30
No breakfast hours and no dinner hours are listed for Executive Dining.

This note does not list menu prices, meal-plan costs, or dish names.
