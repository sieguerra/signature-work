Tide Canteen weekly menu notes

Monday: millet porridge and pickled greens (breakfast); tomato egg noodles (lunch).
Wednesday: the canteen serves dumplings only at lunch, 11:30–13:30. The filling is fennel and tofu; no meat option that day.
Friday: fish-cake soup. The kitchen closes at 19:00 on Fridays, one hour earlier than other weekdays.

Allergy board: sesame is used in the Friday chili oil. The Wednesday dumplings are steamed, not fried.

Payment is harbor credits only. The canteen does not accept the shuttle day pass.
