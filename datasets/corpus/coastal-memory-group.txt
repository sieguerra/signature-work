Coastal Memory Group (fictional research unit)

The Coastal Memory Group studies how harbor towns archive storm records. The group office is Room 214 in the East Archive wing. Weekly seminar: Thursday 16:00–17:00 in Seminar Hut B.

Current working corpus (internal nickname: Salt Ledger) contains 48 digitized logbooks from 1912–1938. The group does not hold records after 1940.

Lead archivist: Jonah Hale. Student liaison: Mei Zhao. External interview requests go to memory@harbor-institute.example, not to personal inboxes.

The group’s public fact sheet states that the 1921 “Green Lamp Flood” lasted 31 hours in Harbor Town, not 48. That correction is deliberate; older tourist plaques are wrong.
