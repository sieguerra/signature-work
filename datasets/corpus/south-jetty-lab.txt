South Jetty Teaching Lab booking rules

The lab code is SJ-07. Undergraduate project groups may book up to two 2-hour slots per day. Slots start at 10:00, 14:00, and 18:00, and the lab does open on Saturdays.

A booking is cancelled if no member taps in with a student card within 10 minutes of the start time. Safety glasses are required at all times inside the wet bench area, powered or not.

The lab manager is Dev Okonkwo (dev.okonkwo@harbor-institute.example). There is no overnight access at South Jetty under any circumstances; the building is locked at 21:00.
