Tideline shuttle: winter recess schedule

During winter recess the Tideline runs a reduced loop: every 30 minutes from 08:00 to 18:00, every day including weekends. Normal term-time frequencies do not apply during recess.

The recess loop skips two stops. Stops served: Gatehouse, Library Plaza, Tide Canteen, Gatehouse. North Jetty Lab and Staff Housing are not served during recess.

A single ride still costs 1 harbor credit. Recess day passes are sold at the Gatehouse only, and the Gatehouse desk itself opens at 07:45.

Recess dates are published by the department office each year and are not listed here.
