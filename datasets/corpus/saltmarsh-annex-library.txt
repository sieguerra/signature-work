Saltmarsh Annex Library hours (term time)

Monday to Friday: 09:00–17:00
Saturday: closed
Sunday: 12:00–18:00

The Annex is a separate building from the Harbor Institute Library and keeps its own schedule. The quiet study floor here is Level 2. There are no group rooms; readers who need one should book at the Harbor Desk instead.

The late-return fine at the Annex is 1 harbor credit per day, capped at 25 credits per item. Bottled water and covered cups are both allowed.

Annex reserves may be borrowed for 48 hours and may leave the building.
